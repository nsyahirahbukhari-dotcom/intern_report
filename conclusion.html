CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Conclusion

In conclusion, the industrial training provided valuable exposure to real working environments and practical software development processes. Throughout the internship period, various tasks related to frontend maintenance, bug fixing, system testing, deployment support, and troubleshooting were successfully carried out. The experience helped strengthen both technical and professional skills while improving understanding of system workflows and development practices.

In addition, the internship enhanced problem-solving abilities, communication skills, teamwork, and adaptability when handling real system issues and working with different environments. Opportunities to work closely with developers and senior staff also provided useful guidance and industry knowledge that contributed to personal and professional growth.

Overall, the industrial training was a meaningful learning experience that increased confidence, improved technical competency, and prepared for future career opportunities in the IT industry.

Recommendations

  • Clear and Simple Workflow Guide – Provide a more understandable workflow or onboarding guide to help new interns/employees quickly understand company processes, system structure, and task flow.

  • Improved Workspace Setup Access – Ensure DEV, SIT, UAT, and PRE-PRD environments are well-prepared and easily accessible (e.g., shared drives, setup guides, or templates) to help new users start work more efficiently.
